### About Me 

Hello! My name is Luca, and I am a first-year Master of Urban and Regional Planning (MURP) student in the Luskin School of Public Affairs at UCLA. I completed my undergrad at UC Davis, studying Political Science and Community Development. My research interests include housing and transportation equity, labor and ethnic studies, intra/intergroup relations, political violence and democratic backsliding, mass incarceration, media analysis, economic development, and inequality. 

For more information, take a look at my website! Feel free to contact me at jalioto@ucla.edu
